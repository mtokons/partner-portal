1. Portal Overview

The SCCG portal is a role-based operational platform. Each user sees features that match their role and current business journey.

Partner Role

Partners use the portal to manage candidates, prepare offers, track tasks, maintain B2B relationships, review finance, access the marketplace, and receive support.

Customer Role

Customers use the portal to review offers, follow their service timeline, track packages and courses, attend sessions, manage messages, and review payments and invoices.

2. Quick Start For New Users

  1. Open the portal URL in a modern browser.
  2. Sign in using the role-specific username and password provided by SCCG.
  3. Wait for the role dashboard to load. This is your home screen.
  4. Use the left menu to move between modules.
  5. Use dashboard summaries first to understand your current tasks, records, or pending actions.
  6. Complete actions step by step and return to the dashboard regularly to monitor progress.

Important: The portal is workflow-driven. Most users should start with dashboard review, then move into records such as candidates, offers, packages, messages, or payments.

3. Partner Role Guide

The partner role is designed for business development, candidate handling, offer preparation, B2B relationship management, financial follow-up, and support communication.

3.1 Partner Dashboard

The dashboard gives a quick operational overview. It is the recommended starting point each time the partner logs in.

Partner dashboard: summary KPIs, shortcuts, and current activity overview.

3.2 Candidate Gallery

Use Candidate Gallery to view, search, and manage candidate records already connected to your account.

  • Review existing candidate records
  • Open candidate details
  • Track status and related service workflows
Candidate Gallery: central place for candidate review and follow-up.

3.3 Create Offer

The Create Offer module is used when the partner wants to prepare service offers for customers or candidates.

  • Create new offers
  • Review offer status
  • Continue working on existing offers
Create Offer: offer preparation and tracking workspace.

3.4 My Tasks

This area helps partners manage action items generated by ongoing services and internal workflows.

My Tasks: operational follow-up for daily work.

3.5 My B2B Network

The B2B section is used to maintain partner company relationships and issue cooperation certificates when relevant.

  • Add B2B companies
  • Upload supporting files
  • Review relationship status
  • Manage certificate actions when available
My B2B Network: relationship records, uploads, and certificate actions.

3.6 Finance

Finance Overview and related pages help partners understand business performance, invoices, payments, refunds, and due amounts.

Finance: overview of financial data and payment-related follow-up.

3.7 Marketplace

The Marketplace provides access to products, services, and downloadable resources relevant to partners.

Marketplace: products, services, and downloadable materials.

3.8 Account Settings and Support

Partners can manage their own account profile and open support interactions when help is needed.

Account Settings: maintain account details.
Support / Helpdesk: submit and monitor support matters.

3.9 Partner Process Flow For New Users

  1. Log in and review the dashboard first.
  2. Open Candidate Gallery to understand your active candidate base.
  3. Create or review offers for current business opportunities.
  4. Check My Tasks to see what requires action today.
  5. Use My B2B Network to maintain business relationships and upload documents where needed.
  6. Check Finance for invoices, payments, and due follow-ups.
  7. Use Marketplace to access partner resources and service packages.
  8. Use Support / Helpdesk whenever a process issue or question needs SCCG follow-up.

4. Customer Role Guide

The customer role is designed to help end users track their journey, documents, services, messages, and payment activity from one place.

4.1 Customer Dashboard

The dashboard is the main home screen for customers. It should be the first page checked after login.

Customer dashboard: summary of service progress and customer activity.

4.2 My Offers

Customers can review offers prepared for them, compare service options, and monitor offer progress.

My Offers: review and follow up on submitted offers.

4.3 My Timeline

The Timeline page helps customers understand what has happened already and what comes next in their service journey.

My Timeline: a structured view of progress and next steps.

4.4 Packages and Courses

Customers can review assigned packages and any learning-related items such as courses.

My Packages: purchased or assigned package details.
My Courses: course access and education-related progress.

4.5 Sessions and Messages

Sessions show scheduled engagements while Messages is the communication space for direct follow-up.

Sessions: customer appointments and planned interactions.
Messages: communication and response history.

4.6 Payments and Invoices

These pages give financial transparency for the customer side of the journey.

Payments: review payment activity and outstanding items.
Invoices: billing history and invoice review.

4.7 Customer Process Flow For New Users

  1. Log in and start with the dashboard.
  2. Review My Offers to understand approved, pending, or active services.
  3. Check My Timeline to understand the current stage of your journey.
  4. Open My Packages and My Courses to review active learning or service items.
  5. Monitor Sessions for scheduled support or educational interactions.
  6. Use Messages for communication and clarification with the support team.
  7. Review Payments and Invoices regularly for financial clarity.

5. End-to-End Process Flows

5.1 Partner End-to-End Flow

  1. Partner logs in.
  2. Partner checks dashboard and active tasks.
  3. Partner reviews or creates candidate records.
  4. Partner prepares offers and continues candidate service workflows.
  5. Partner updates B2B relationships and uploads supporting files where necessary.
  6. Partner monitors financial progress, invoices, and payment activity.
  7. Partner uses support when escalation or clarification is needed.

5.2 Customer End-to-End Flow

  1. Customer logs in.
  2. Customer checks dashboard summary.
  3. Customer reviews offers and timeline updates.
  4. Customer accesses packages, courses, sessions, and messages.
  5. Customer checks payments and invoices as part of ongoing service delivery.
  6. Customer returns to the portal regularly to track new updates.

6. Good Usage Practices

  • Start from the dashboard every session.
  • Use the left navigation menu rather than relying only on browser history.
  • Review tasks, messages, and timeline items frequently.
  • Keep profile and account information accurate.
  • Use support when any business or technical question blocks your work.

7. Troubleshooting and Support

  • If a page does not load correctly, refresh the browser once.
  • If login fails, verify the exact username, password, and correct portal login URL.
  • If expected records are missing, first return to the dashboard and then re-open the module.
  • If you still face an issue, use the Support / Helpdesk area or contact SCCG directly.
